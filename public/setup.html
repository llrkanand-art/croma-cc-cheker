Session Sync Setup

1. Neeche wale link ko long-press karke Copy link address karo.
2. Chrome me Bookmarks → Add karo, naam "Sync Croma" rakho, URL me wo paste karo.
3. croma.com/cart kholo (login + cart me product hona chahiye) → Bookmarks me "Sync Croma" tap karo.
4. "Session synced!" aane ke baad app khol ke START maar do.
